Our Curriculum

Shaping Well-Rounded Learners

We follow the Matriculation syllabus with a balanced focus on academics, values and life skills — ensuring every child grows intellectually and as a person.

01

Kindergarten Level

Activity-based learning introduced from Kindergarten level to promote curiosity, creativity and a love for learning in the early years.

02

Strong Foundation

Strong foundation built in Languages, Mathematics, Sciences and Social Sciences for Classes I–V, with a focus on conceptual clarity.

03

Second Languages

Second languages offered: Tamil, Hindi and French from Class V onwards to develop multilingual abilities and cultural appreciation.

04

Communication Skills

Special focus on spoken English, communication skills and personality development through dedicated programs and workshops.

05

Board Exam Preparation

Structured coaching for Class X and XII board exams with model tests, revision sessions and personalized mentoring for each student.

06

Co-Curricular Activities

Mandatory participation in sports, arts, cultural events and leadership programs alongside academics for all-round development.
