A Green Oasis of Learning

Akshaya Vidyalaya is set amidst the serene surroundings of a 25-acre green campus at Seelapadi, Dindigul — providing a perfect learning environment away from the city.

Green Campus

A sprawling 25-acre pollution-free campus with lush gardens, open spaces and natural surroundings that create a calm and inspiring learning environment for students.

  • ✓ Clean air for healthy learning
