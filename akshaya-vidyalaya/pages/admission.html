How to Enroll Your Child

We welcome students who are eager to learn, grow and excel. Below is a step-by-step guide to our admission process and eligibility criteria.

📋 Admission Procedure

  • The academic year begins in June and ends in April.
  • Admission forms and school prospectus can be purchased from the school office by paying ₹300.
  • Admission is primarily based on an Entrance Examination in Mathematics, English and Science, conducted in April/May.
  • Students are tested at the level of their previously completed class.
  • Priority is given to parents who register their children early.
  • Admission into Class XI is limited and subject to availability of seats.
  • The Principal reserves the right to accept or reject an application without assigning reasons.

✅ Eligibility Criteria

  • Kindergarten (KG): Age-appropriate admission as per government guidelines.
  • Primary Classes (I–V): Successful completion of the previous grade with transfer certificate and progress card.
  • Middle & Secondary (VI–X): Admission subject to performance in the entrance examination and availability of seats.
  • Higher Secondary (XI–XII): Selection based on academic performance in Class X, entrance test and subject-stream preference.
  • Students must submit all required documents: Birth Certificate, Community Certificate (if applicable), Transfer Certificate, Progress Report and Passport-sized photographs.

🔄 Withdrawal Policy

  • Parents intending to withdraw their child must submit a written application to the school office.
  • Transfer Certificate (T.C.) will be issued only after settlement of all dues and clearance of library, laboratory and school property.
  • One month's notice period or fee in lieu of notice is mandatory for withdrawal.
  • The school reserves the right to request withdrawal of a student on grounds of irregular attendance, unsatisfactory progress, indiscipline, or non-payment of fees.
  • Admission fees and other charges once paid are non-refundable.
