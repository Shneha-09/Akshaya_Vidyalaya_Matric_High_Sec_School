Science Labs

State-of-the-Art Laboratories

Our labs are equipped with modern instruments and safety equipment, enabling students to learn through hands-on experiments and practical exploration.

Chemistry Laboratory

Our Chemistry Lab provides a safe and stimulating environment for students to explore chemical reactions through structured experiments aligned with the Matriculation syllabus.

  • Well-equipped with modern apparatus and safety measures.
  • Enables students to perform experiments and understand chemical reactions practically.
  • Dedicated lab assistants guide students through experiments safely.
  • Comprehensive stock of chemicals and reagents for all syllabi experiments.
  • First-aid kit and fire safety equipment always available.

Biology Laboratory

The Biology Lab is designed to bring the study of life sciences alive, with specimens, models and microscopes that allow students to examine the natural world up close.

  • Spacious and furnished with microscopes, models, charts and specimens.
  • Encourages hands-on learning of human anatomy, botany and zoology concepts.
  • Well-maintained specimen collection for practical learning.
  • Periodic dissection sessions under expert supervision.
  • Digital projection for detailed biological diagrams and videos.

Physics Laboratory

The Physics Lab bridges theory and practice, allowing students to conduct experiments in optics, mechanics, electricity and magnetism using advanced instruments.

  • Stocked with advanced instruments for optics, mechanics and electronics experiments.
  • Provides experiential learning to strengthen theoretical knowledge.
  • Regular practical classes aligned with board exam requirements.
  • Safety equipment and trained lab staff ensure a secure environment.
  • Optics benches, galvanometers and electrical kits available.

Computer Laboratory

Our state-of-the-art computer lab equips students with essential digital skills, coding knowledge and internet literacy, preparing them for a technology-driven future.

  • 60+ modern computer workstations with latest software.
  • High-speed broadband internet connectivity throughout the lab.
  • Programming and coding classes for all standards.
  • Dedicated trained IT faculty for every session.
