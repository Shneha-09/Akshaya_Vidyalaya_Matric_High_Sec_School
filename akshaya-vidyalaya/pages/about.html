About Our AVMHSS

Akshaya Vidyalaya Matric Higher Secondary School

Founded in June 1994, Akshaya Vidyalaya Matriculation Higher Secondary School has steadily grown into one of the most respected institutions in Dindigul district. Spread across a sprawling 25-acre green campus in Seelapadi, the school offers a serene and pollution-free learning environment, away from the hustle and bustle of the city.

Guided by the vision of Dr. E.N. Palanisamy's Group of Education, the school has created a unique identity by combining traditional Gurukulam values with contemporary teaching methods. Over the decades, Akshaya Vidyalaya has produced outstanding results in both academic and co-curricular activities.

The school takes pride in its dedicated faculty, modern infrastructure and student-friendly environment. With a balanced focus on academics, sports, arts and life skills, Akshaya Vidyalaya ensures all-round development of every child.

Today, Akshaya Vidyalaya stands as a temple of learning, where thousands of students have realized their dreams and stepped into successful futures.

Trust Name & Year of Establishment

Akshaya Vidyalaya Matriculation Higher Secondary School is administered by the Akshaya Vidyalaya Charitable Trust, which was established in the year 1997 with the aim of creating a strong foundation for value-based education in Dindigul.

Over the years, the Trust has expanded its contribution to education by setting up and managing multiple institutions under the umbrella of Dr. E.N. Palanisamy's Group of Education. Its mission has always been to blend traditional values with modern academic excellence.
